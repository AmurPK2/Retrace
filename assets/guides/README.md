# Norwegian video guides

Open **Study → Se videoguidene** inside RETRACE. Both videos are bundled and work offline, with Norwegian narration, large on-screen explanations, optional Norwegian subtitles and fullscreen controls.

- **C1, C2 og C3 – enkelt forklart:** candle body and wick, timeframe, the order of C1/C2/C3, saving an idea and revealing the genuine continuation.
- **Slik bruker du RETRACE:** replay controls, drawing and Shift axis locking, orders and risk, trade arrows, saved decision charts, AI review and learning.

The visuals are edited actual RETRACE window screenshots, plus a timed sequence captured during real replay. They use genuine historical XAUUSD prices in an isolated simulated demonstration account. Charts were not generated, interpolated or redrawn. Labels, crops and captions explain the actual captured chart. Example trades are simulated and do not demonstrate profitability. The AI scene shows the app's explicitly labelled saved-data fallback after an unverifiable draft.

Narration: Norwegian Bokmål, Microsoft Pernille neural voice, generated from the guide script using edge-tts. MP4: H.264/AAC, 1920×1080; matching WebVTT captions and JPEG posters are included.
