RETRACE for Windows

Extract the entire ZIP, then open RETRACE.exe.
First setup downloads real starter chart data and about 3.4 GB of local AI files.
Keep the app open to finish. Downloads resume after an interruption.
No LM Studio, AI account, API key or Discord application setup is needed.

Discord desktop: Balance, Wins and Check out the project appear automatically.
Sharing can be turned off in RETRACE Settings. Discord privacy settings apply.
Learning: select Norsk or English. C1/C2/C3 are three candles in order.
Trades: the Trades button or History > Show on chart finds entry/exit arrows.

Your sessions, prices and AI files stay in the local data folder beside the app.
Keep that data folder when updating. Back it up separately.
Telegram is optional and has no preconfigured sources or accounts.
This is a simulated replay app; there is no broker order route.

Source, updates and instructions: https://github.com/AmurPK2/Retrace

Video guides: Study > Se videoguidene. Two Norwegian guides with real charts,
narration, subtitles and fullscreen controls work offline.
Draw a trend line or ray while holding Shift to lock horizontally or vertically.
Release Shift to draw freely; Enter also completes a pending two-point line.
