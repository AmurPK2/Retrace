RETRACE 0.3.0 for Windows

Extract the entire ZIP, then open RETRACE.exe. First setup downloads genuine
starter gold history and about 3.4 GB of pinned, hash-checked local AI files.
Keep the app open. Interrupted downloads resume. Provider throttling may delay
price downloads; missing prices are never invented. No AI key/server command
or Discord Developer Portal setup is required. Windows x64; 16 GB RAM advised.

Right-click the chart for a new order draft. ENTRY, SL and TP move separately.
Space / Right Arrow: next candle. P: play/pause. Fields and video guides capture
their own keys. All trades are simulated; there is no broker order route.

Study > Se videoguidene: six Norwegian offline guides with voice, subtitles
and fullscreen. New session: free practice or ten beginner challenges.
HTF candles: original side-candle view. Objects: saved drawing management.

XAUUSD, EURAUD and NAS100 CFD have separate feeds/specifications. EURAUD uses
an AUD practice account. NQ futures need a separate licensed source and are
not enabled. NAS100 CFD is not NQ. AI answers can still be wrong; computed
replies and fallbacks are labelled. No strategy or profit is guaranteed.

Discord desktop: Balance, Trades taken, RT icon and Check out the project.
Turn sharing off in Settings. Discord's activity/privacy settings still apply.

Keep your data folder when updating; back it up separately. It contains your
sessions and settings. Prices/model files stay local and are not in the ZIP.
Telegram is optional, starts empty, and uses only your own credentials/sources.

Source, releases and documentation: https://github.com/AmurPK2/Retrace
