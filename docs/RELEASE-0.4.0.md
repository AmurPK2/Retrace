Attempts, committed answers, factual checks and demonstrated understanding are distinct; revealing a candle or earning profit cannot award mastery. Existing v2 answers and RR studies remain readable without being rewritten into new passes.

The coach receives explicit per-entry risk and candle references, and abstains when decision-time historical evidence is incomplete or corrupt. These changes preserve the single forming 4H candle on the main chart price scale and the separate XAUUSD, EURAUD and NAS100 feeds. NQ remains removed.

All six Norwegian guides show the integrated UI and retain offline audio, captions and fullscreen. The risk guide distinguishes one R (original cash risk) from the planned reward/risk ratio. No guide claims a proven strategy or winning probability.
