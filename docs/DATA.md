# Data, units and execution

RETRACE downloads historical bid/ask ticks from [Dukascopy's public historical feed](https://www.dukascopy.com/swiss/english/marketwatch/historical/). Data is historical, not a live quote service. The release contains no market dataset. Coverage is shown per instrument in Data hours; failed or corrupt intervals remain explicit and pause replay execution.

- XAUUSD maps to XAUUSD (price divisor 1,000), USD account, 100 oz per lot, minimum/step 0.01 lot, quote increment 0.001, simulated leverage 1:500.
- EURAUD maps to EURAUD (divisor 100,000), AUD account, 100,000 EUR per lot, minimum/step 0.01 lot, quote increment 0.00001, simulated leverage 1:30. P&L is directly in AUD; the app does not silently call it USD or invent an AUD/USD conversion.
- NAS100 maps to Dukascopy USATECHIDXUSD (divisor 1,000). It is a US technology index CFD feed, with one CFD unit worth USD 1 per index point; minimum/step one unit, stored quote increment 0.001, simulated leverage 1:20. Its new practice account starts at USD 10,000 to cover meaningful index-unit margin. This is a simulator assumption, not a quoted broker offer.
- NQ futures are outside this release and have been removed from instrument choices. NAS100 is explicitly a CFD feed.

Contract and symbol references: [Dukascopy instruments](https://www.dukascopy.com/client/javadoc3/com/dukascopy/api/Instrument.html), [Dukascopy CFD market specifications](https://www.dukascopy.com/swiss/english/cfd/range-of-markets/?lang=en), [CME E-mini Nasdaq-100 contract specifications](https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/equities/nasdaq/e-mini-nasdaq-100.contractSpecs.html).

Gross P&L = direction × (exit − entry) × quantity × contract size. Buys enter at ask and exit at bid; sells reverse that. Bid/ask spreads are therefore included. Commission, slippage, swap and stop-out assumptions are configurable per session. Default explicit commission/slippage/swap are zero, not an assertion that trading is cost-free. Margin follows the instrument's account currency and contract/leverage assumptions. Stops may fill worse after a real price gap.

Calendars are evaluated in America/New_York with DST: FX closes Friday 17:00 and reopens Sunday 17:00; gold has a weekday 17:00–18:00 break; the configured NAS100 CFD break is 16:15–18:00. A whole hour is classified closed only when its beginning and end are closed. Exceptional holidays are not guessed; missing holiday data remains unavailable rather than tradable. UTC is the storage clock; Europe/Oslo can format labels.

The importer validates, hashes and records source chunks, retries with bounded backoff, and resumes completed ranges. Downloads are serialized with a polite delay; 429/503 responses trigger a cooldown. Empty/corrupt/unavailable open-market intervals are not filled with synthetic ticks. Normal market closures use thin separators on compressed chart time. Genuine price discontinuities remain. Next candle processes every intervening tick, including entries and protective exits.

Drawings retain timestamp/price anchors and dataset/session ownership. Compressed-time mapping interpolates against actual adjacent candle timestamps. Loaded drawing/history context is bounded to 90 days / 130,000 bars; this is not an unlimited-history claim. Existing gold sessions retain their original specifications and data roots.


## 16 November 2026 cache repair and assessment quality

Whole-hour replacement now deletes the preceding minute/hour aggregates in the same database transaction. A shorter repaired hour, a failed download or a partial import cannot leave old candles behind as current data. Reading a cache validates its compressed hash, decoded prices/volumes, timestamp order, UTC-hour membership and recorded count/first/last metadata.

A corrupt ready cache no longer consumes the import pass merely by being relabelled missing. The same pass tries original BI5 bytes only when their recorded SHA-256 matches and decoding and interval coverage pass; otherwise it makes the existing bounded source request. Existing head/tail gaps are recomputed even for old chunks that lack gap metadata. The latest-day probe also continues to the original source if its cached noon chunk is corrupt. There are no synthetic replacement ticks. Duplicate equal-time paths retain original ordering.

Coach requests verify the actual evidence window through its decision cutoff, including selected C1/C2 and HTF context. Unknown/partial/corrupt hours, invalid source rows and more than five open-market minutes without tick evidence make assessment unavailable. A later gap in the same stored hour does not automatically invalidate an earlier decision cutoff. This is conservative data sufficiency, not a claim that every quiet interval contained trades. Existing replay execution still refuses unknown gaps; a new integration test verifies that a pending order cannot acquire a false fill across a missing hour. No trading or challenge policy was changed by the small worker integration.

Recurring closures remain instrument-specific. A saved `closure` marker is not accepted for the coach merely because it exists: it must match that instrument's supported recurring schedule. An invalid old closure is fetched again during import. The source [Dukascopy trading-session reference](https://wiki.dukascopy.com/en/manuals/jforex4-desktop/trading-sessions) confirms gold's New York session and the US Tech index's Chicago session (equivalent configured New York hours). The [official holiday calendar](https://www.dukascopy.com/swiss/english/marketwatch/trading-breaks-calendar/) was inspected, but its public widget returned HTTP 403 during this task. No exact historical holiday interval was available for inclusion, so exceptional-calendar coverage remains incomplete. An empty open-hour response/404 stays **unknown**, not a guessed holiday or tradable hour. Do not infer a complete annual holiday calendar from the recurring schedule.

Original-byte recovery and source-refetch paths passed isolated BI5 fixture tests, including hash failure and old metadata. A separate attempt to obtain a real public XAUUSD source hour did not complete, and a bounded request timed out after 20 seconds. **No fresh real-feed recovery or complete instrument coverage was verified in this workstream.** No user market database/session was opened or repaired. Re-run `scripts/verify-coach-data.ts` in an isolated profile when the provider is available; its source attempt is bounded and records unavailable status honestly. NQ remains removed.
